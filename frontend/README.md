# Sylo AI 🚀
> Decentralized, AI-Powered Enterprise Project Execution & Micro-Credentialing Platform

Sylo AI bridges the gap between enterprise R&D sponsors and student innovation teams through cryptographic smart contracts, automated AI scoping, air-gapped plagiarism shields, and tamper-evident ledgers.

---

## 🏗️ Architecture & Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, React Router DOM, Lucide React, `qrcode.react`
* **Backend Integration:** Kotlin (Ktor/Spring Boot) & Supabase (PostgreSQL, Row-Level Security, Triggers)
* **AI Engine:** Gemini & local Ollama models for automated scoping and AST similarity matching.

---

## ✨ Core Modules & Workflow
1. **Sponsor Problem Posting:** Secure brief publishing with public summaries and locked confidential IP layers.
2. **Student Bidding & AI Scoping:** Automated translation of briefs into 3-phase milestone charters via AI collaboration.
3. **Cryptographic Project Charter:** 9-clause immutable agreement requiring dual digital signatures.
4. **Zero-Fee Escrow Engine:** Transparent fund distribution (75% student impact share, 15% mentor share, 10% compute reserve, 0% platform fee).
5. **Plagiarism Shield:** Air-gapped AST similarity scanner ensuring deliverables score <70% before release.
6. **Live Ledger & Audit Panel:** Real-time SHA-256 fingerprint logging with block integrity verification.
7. **QR Credential Verification:** Instantly verifiable proof of work for resumes and LinkedIn.
8. **Dispute Resolution & Job Placements:** Automated escrow freeze on grievance, and direct corporate placement unlocks.
